Privacy policy

Last updated November 19, 2026.

BrainOS is a private tool used only by its owner, Alex Gonzalez, with his own Google accounts.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, BrainOS reads and drafts in the owner's Gmail, and reads and edits the owner's Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Tasks and Contacts through Google's APIs.

Where data goes

Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer. Google data is processed only to answer the owner's own requests. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Google API Services

BrainOS's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
